Lantern

Before you, night was only dark.
Then you came, soft as a lamp let down,
and every quiet street I knew
learned how to glow.
I would walk them all again
just to find you at the end.

— always yours

Serenade

If I were a song, I would be slow,
a waltz the evening hums to itself.
I would climb the air to your window
and say what words are too small to say:
stay a while, and let the night be long.

— with love

Vow

I promise you the small things first:
warm hands, an open door, the last of the tea.
Then the larger ones, one by one,
as many as there are stars to share,
and every one of them yours.

— forever

Thank you sa pagsagot, Jai.

Pwede ba kitang isayaw?

I hope you enjoyed my version of a serenade, Jai.

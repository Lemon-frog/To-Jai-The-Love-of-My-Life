✦ Poetic Vows ✦

Lantern

I hope I fill your days with warmth,
like the gentle light of the morning sun.
I hope I fill your nights with peace,
like the soft whisper of a breeze.
I hope that life embraces you as gently as I do,
because you are the the lantern that brightens my world,
and the warmth I hold.

- always yours

Serenade

From the moment I heard you,
I knew that you were the only one I would listen to.
You were a familiar melody,
so vivid, yet so far.
A rhythm that stays in one's mind,
long after the music has ended.
A song like you is hard to find,
so please, dance with me for a lifetime.

- with love

Vow

I promise you a love for the ages,
so pure and intentional that there is no room for doubt.
Come to me sad, angry, happy,
or whatever it is you feel in that moment,
and I promise to accept every last bit of you.
For I have chosen you, and I will choose you, every day for the rest of my life.

- now & forever

Thank you sa pagsagot, Jai.
